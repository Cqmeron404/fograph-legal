One sentence a day. Here's how it works, and how to reach me.

Writing a check-in

Open Fograph, write one sentence about today in the box, and tap Save check-in. It's stamped with the date and time you wrote it. To change it, tap Edit on the saved card — you can update today's entry as often as you like.

The 7:00 AM reminder

Fograph asks for notification permission the first time you open it. Say yes and you'll get one nudge at 7:00 AM. If you've already checked in that day, the reminder skips itself. Change it any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Fograph.

Where my entries live

On your device. Fograph has no account and no server, so nothing is uploaded. Entries are included in a normal iCloud or computer backup, and they're removed if you delete the app.

Common questions

  • Why only one sentence? The 140-character limit is the point — it keeps the habit small enough to keep.
  • Can I write for a past day? No. Fograph is a daily check-in, so it only records today.
  • I missed a day. Did I lose my streak? The streak counts consecutive days ending today, so it restarts after a gap. Your log keeps every entry either way.
