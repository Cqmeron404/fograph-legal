Fograph

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Fograph is a daily journal that keeps one sentence per day. It is built to work entirely on your device, and this policy explains exactly what that means.

What Fograph collects

Nothing. Fograph does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • Your check-ins are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server for Fograph to send data to.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDK in the app.

Notifications

Fograph can show a daily reminder at 7:00 AM. That reminder is scheduled locally by iOS on your device. If you have already written your check-in for the day, the reminder is skipped. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Fograph.

Quotes and verses

The daily quote and Bible verse are included in the app itself. No content is downloaded at runtime, and no request is made when you open Fograph.

Your entries

You control your writing. Entries stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, not by Fograph.

Children

Fograph collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
